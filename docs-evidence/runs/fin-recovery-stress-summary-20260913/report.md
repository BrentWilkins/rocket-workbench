# Full recovery uncertainty comparison

Three designs × 288 deterministic cases = 864 flights. 192 planned dummy/logger and 96 diagnostic-empty cases per
design. These are not reliability probabilities. No CFD acceptance or physical launch clearance is implied.

| delta-chute24-body500-nominal | 192                  | 142.97–180.36     | 49.89–58.51      | 3.76–4.95          |

Inputs: nominal/upper avionics, mount mass ×1/1.5, printed mass ×1/1.2, payload CG ±5 mm, chute Cd 0.6/0.9, winds 0/2/4
m/s. Canopy mass and packed volume are provisional area-scaled estimates.
