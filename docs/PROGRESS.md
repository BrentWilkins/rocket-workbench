Updated 2026-09-13. Start at [current designs](CURRENT_DESIGN.md), not an earlier study report.

- Corrected geometry/nose search: 54 geometries and 1,620 completed flights.
- Three-option uncertainty comparison: 432 completed flights.
- Selected cone at nominal/upper avionics mass: 60 completed flights.
- Matching CAD, flight models, component renders, print-orientation screen and six-part V5 X1C project are available.

  study. There are no simulated 24 mm results or servo-equipped designs yet.
