# Current project status

The [current recommendation](CURRENT_DESIGN.md) is a **530 mm BT-60 body, 40 mm ogive and the existing 27 g collar**, carrying the camera/logger payload on D12-5 or E12-6. It remains **not flight-cleared**; existing manufacturing artifacts still describe the 500 mm baseline.

- [Flight robustness report](FLIGHT_ROBUSTNESS.md): 12,000 paired uncertainty flights, 120 deterministic cases and 108 RocketPy ascent cross-checks. The longer body gains stability margin for a small altitude cost, without establishing better wind handling.
- Launch and recovery remain the priorities: 36-inch-guide samples miss the 12 m/s departure screen; longer-guide upper-mass cases still exceed the 10 m/s deployment screen in 6 m/s wind.
- [CAD renders and CFD](CFD_LATEST_DESIGN.md): current-candidate imagery and exploratory computed streamlines. No validated drag correction or camera/pressure-port comparison is claimed.
- Next: update the selected build configuration/CAD, dry-fit hardware, measure finished mass/CG, verify guide compatibility and recovery, then rerun with measured inputs and launch conditions.

See the [measurement checklist](MEASUREMENTS.md), [re-verification checklist](REVERIFICATION.md) and [archive](ARCHIVE.md). Earlier pass counts belong to their earlier geometry, hardware and assumptions, not the current build decision.
