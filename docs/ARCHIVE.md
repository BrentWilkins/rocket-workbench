# Archive — old news, not current designs

These documents and their linked study reports preserve earlier work. **Do not use their dimensions, thresholds,
rankings, print projects or prices as current instructions.** Old URLs remain usable for provenance, but archived pages
are labeled and excluded from site search. The sealed simulation evidence is not rewritten.

Use [current designs](CURRENT_DESIGN.md), [current hardware](AVIONICS_DESIGN.md), [build instructions](BUILD.md), and
the [next-platform decision](NEXT_PLATFORM.md) instead.
